PureCrest

Video editor. AI Video Strategist. Creative Strategist. [ Project 2025 ]

Project Overview

Purecrest is a specialized dermatological skincare brand offering targeted skin fold treatments formulated specifically for menopausal skin concerns. Its identity blends clinical authority with empathetic, science-backed solutions, creating a reassuring and trustworthy brand experience. The product positioning and packaging reflect simplicity, efficacy, and a targeted approach to eliminating trapped moisture and fungal irritation.

Brief

Create a direct-response, educational social media video ad for Purecrest that destigmatizes menopausal skin fold rashes (intertrigo) while explaining the biological root cause behind recurring odor and irritation. The video should establish clinical credibility and emotional reassurance through a conversational, dermatologist-led delivery, supported by visual diagrams, before-and-after results, and clear product demonstration, seamlessly transitioning from problem education to a low-risk, money-back guaranteed call to action.

Tutti Frutti Cafe

Video Editor. AI Strategist. Creative Strategist. [ Project 2025 ]

Project Overview

Tutti Frutti Café is an evolved hospitality and dessert brand expanding from its nearly 20-year global legacy as a self-serve frozen yogurt pioneer across 28 countries and nearly 700 stores into an all-day social café destination. Its identity blends a vibrant, nostalgic heritage with modern lifestyle dining, creating a warm, community-driven brand experience. The new café concept reflects versatility, inclusivity, and a forward-looking approach to casual dining and social gathering.

Brief

Create a brand evolution and franchise recruitment video for Tutti Frutti Café that introduces its expanded all-day menu and modern café format to prospective franchisees, area developers, and investors. The video should balance established global credibility with contemporary growth potential through a founder-led narrative, lifestyle B-roll of diverse food and beverage offerings, and clear on-screen callouts of business models and dayparts, positioning the brand as a scalable, community-centered partnership opportunity.
